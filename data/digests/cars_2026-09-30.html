How a "top deal" is decided: a listing qualifies only when its asking price is at least 15.0% and €500 below the median of at least 4 distinct comparable current asking prices. Comparables must match on make/model (incl. generation), fuel, year ±2, mileage ±60,000 km, engine ±0.15L, and gearbox/body whenever both sides state them. Score is 0–100: 50 at the median, +1 point per 1% cheaper, up to +10 points for mileage below the pool's median (scaled across the ±60,000 km window) and +3 points per year newer than the pool's median — so a cheap-but-worn car ranks below a cheap-and-fresh one.
