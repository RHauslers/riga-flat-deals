Score is 0–100: 50 at the median, +1 point per 1% cheaper, up to +10 points for mileage below the pool's median (scaled across the ±60,000 km window) and +3 points per year newer than the pool's median — so a cheap-but-worn car ranks below a cheap-and-fresh one.
